Project planner for architects & engineers

Land map with true-north direction, 16-zone Vastu compass, apartment or commercial floor plate, FAR / coverage / parking checks and an engineering summary. For villas use the Auto plan tab.

Irregular plots use the largest rectangle that fits inside the boundary with the biggest setback. Corners are named A (top-left), B (top-right), C (bottom-right), D (bottom-left). Byelaws differ by city, so confirm FAR, coverage, setbacks and parking with your local authority.

All engineering figures are early-stage thumb rules. Final design must be by a licensed structural engineer (IS 456, IS 1893, IS 875, NBC 2016).

📏 Land survey: measure by GPS or satellite map, then print the report

Search a place, tap the plot corners on the satellite map (or stand at each corner and press the GPS button), then fill the client details. East, West, North and South boundaries and side lengths are worked out for you.

1. Find the land and mark its corners

GPS off · Drag a corner to adjust it · Right-click a corner or use ✕ in the table to delete it · Go around the plot in order (clockwise or anticlockwise). Phone GPS is usually accurate to 3–10 m; for registry-grade work use a licensed surveyor (Amin) with a total station or DGPS.

2. Boundaries (chauhaddi)

Write who or what is on each side of the plot. Lengths come from the map automatically.
